Cosmic Math Equations

Depending on your stellar parameters, diameter is mapped through these properties:

  • From Radius: $d = 2r$
  • From Circumference: $d = \frac{C}{\pi}$
  • From Area: $d = 2 \times \sqrt{\frac{A}{\pi}}$
